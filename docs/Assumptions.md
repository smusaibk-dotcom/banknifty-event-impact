# Limitations/Assumptions

- The analysis establishes association rather than causal attribution.

- Earnings announcements occur alongside other market events, so the observed Banknifty movement cannot be attributed exclusively to earnings.

- Earnings announcement can occur during/after the market hours which can significantly affect the reaction observation.

- It is Assumed that Constituent weights change only due to price movement of the constituent and other thing being constant. However, Constituent weights change over time due to multiple factors other than price change only such as Stock split, market-cap etc.

- The daily weight reconstruction on session close basis considering the weight to be static for the day whereas the actual weights keep on varying real-time durig market hours itself.

- The relationship between earnings surprise and market movement was not strong enough in the analysed data to support a simple surprise-based explanation.

- Historical movement patterns do not by themselves establish future predictive performance.

- The analysis focuses on the available study period from January 2023 through August 2026 which is a short duration, each bank recording approx ~15 events, for such analysis.
